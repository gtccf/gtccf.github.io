Who we are


By now you’ve probably realized that there are a million things to know about this campus and its people... and all of that can feel a little overwhelming at times. Thankfully, there are a ton of people who are already rooting for you! We at CCF (Christian Campus Fellowship) are some of those people.

We are a diverse campus ministry, full of people from different backgrounds and beliefs, whose goal is to live like Jesus and show others that they are loved. Our community aims to treat all people with dignity and respect, and we hope that our hospitality says enough about who we are more than any words could. CCF is a home away from home for many students, and we’d love to be yours, too.
Every week we’ll have tons of opportunities to get involved, so stop by whenever to get better connected with our community!

Meet the Freshmen Interns!


CCF is run by a staff of Georgia Tech alumni, as well as a team of interns who dedicate their year to serving Jesus through the CCF community. We have a handful interns who work exclusively with the freshmen in our ministry, so read below to learn more about them!




Mary Chris Cella | Freshmen Intern | marychris.cella@gtccf.org


Hometown: Smyrna, GA
Major: Business
Fun Fact: I’ve been on a game show

I started going to CCF my freshman year and was immediately drawn in by a community that encouraged me to ask questions, build meaningful relationships, and, most importantly, grow in my relationship with Jesus. Over the last four years, I’ve been poured into by staff, interns, and fellow students, while also having the opportunity to pour into others as a student leader. Walking alongside students and seeing God work in this community has been the most impactful part of my college experience and ultimately led me to become an intern. I’m excited to spend this next year building and investing in this ministry that has meant so much to me.



Becca Fisher | Freshmen Intern | becca.fisher@gtccf.org


Hometown: Moreland, GA
Fun Fact: I grew up on a farm

Ten days after graduating with a BS in Biomedical Engineering, I flew to Sierra Leone. For nine weeks, I worked with a water company dedicated to providing jobs and clean water. Sierra Leone showed me that problem-solving is not only for projects, but for people as well. I love asking deep questions to better understand how God’s people and creation connect. In May 2026, I graduated with my master’s in Management while also working part-time for a nonprofit and leadership coaching program. Throughout all of this, CCF has been a compass, pointing me to Jesus through community, stories, and dreams. I am excited to do the same for students.
